Controls

Gamepad: left stick flies, right stick X is rudder and Y is throttle, LT brakes, A/Y flaps, X autopilot, B camera. Touch: the left pad is the stick (pull down to climb), and the slider on the right is the throttle.

Crashed

Enter restarts. V replays the last minute at any time.
